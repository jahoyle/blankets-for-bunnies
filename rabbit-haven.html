About the organization

RABBIT HAVEN

Rabbit Haven is a volunteer-run, no-kill rabbit rescue in Gig Harbor dedicated to giving every bunny the care, space, and quality of life they deserve. Founded out of a need for better housing, nutrition, medical care, and education for domestic rabbits, Rabbit Haven provides a safe place for unwanted, abandoned, and mistreated rabbits to recover and receive the specialized care they need. Every bunny arrives with their own history, and beyond medical evaluation and treatment, rabbits are given proper housing, enrichment, socialization, and the time and attention needed to feel safe again—whether their stay lasts a few months or several years.

What makes Rabbit Haven especially meaningful is its commitment to treating rabbits as individuals, each with their own personalities, relationships, needs, and preferences. Bonded rabbits stay together, shy rabbits are given the patience and attention they need to learn to trust again, and special-needs rabbits receive care tailored to their individual circumstances. Some become lifelong residents when another rehoming would not be in their best interest, living in spacious enclosures with opportunities to free-roam.

For rabbits who have made it clear that life with humans simply isn’t for them, Rabbit Haven also has a protected outdoor warren providing a safe alternative where they can live more independently while still receiving food, clean water, vaccinations, monitoring, and veterinary care. Even after rabbits are adopted, Rabbit Haven remains committed to them requiring adoptors to return a rabbit to the rescue if they can no longer care for them. Rather than asking every rabbit to fit into the same model, Rabbit Haven works to give each one the life that fits them.
