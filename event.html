BLANKETS FOR BUNNIES

Let’s help make rescue rabbits a little cozier this winter! Join us for a drop-in volunteer event to make fleece tie blankets for Rabbit Haven, a volunteer-run rabbit rescue and adoption center in Gig Harbor, Washington.

Date: October 21
Time: 9:00 a.m.–12:00 p.m. — drop in whenever you can!
Location: Building 87, Room 1004
Recommended time: About 30 minutes—enough to finish one blanket
Our goal: 50 blankets!

What we’ll be doing

We’ll create approximately 24" × 24" double-layer fleece tie blankets to give rescued rabbits soft, washable beds. Simply cut the fleece, fringe the edges, and tie the layers together- no sewing, needles, thread, or experience needed!

Why it matters

Rabbit Haven provides shelter, medical care, and individual attention to abandoned and surrendered domestic rabbits, including special-needs rabbits and lifelong residents. These blankets help make their stays more comfortable—and because bunnies love to chew, dig, and “redesign” their bedding, a steady supply is especially valuable.

Every bit of help counts

Make a whole blanket, team up with a colleague, start one for the next person, or stay and make a few. If cutting and tying aren’t your thing, we’d still love some enthusiastic supervisors and vibe-bringers!

Come for the time you have available, connect with teammates, and help us reach our goal of 50 blankets.
