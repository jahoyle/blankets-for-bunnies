HOW TO MAKE A BLANKET...

  1. Stack both layers of fleece on top of one another

  2. remove any factory edges from the fleece

  3. Cut a 4”x4” square from each corner

  4. Cut 1” strips around the entire perimeter of the fleece

  5. Tie the strips from the opposite layers together

  6. Voila!
